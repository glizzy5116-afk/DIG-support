# DIG: Mini Excavator Level

DIG is a simple guidance aid for mini excavators. Fit a WitMotion WT901BLECL sensor or a spare iPhone (the **sensor phone**) to the bucket, and keep your everyday iPhone in the cab (the **cab phone**). Additional sensors on the boom and stick give the highest accuracy. No spare phone? Use WitMotion sensors throughout.

**Getting started**
1. Install DIG on your cab phone, and on the spare iPhone if you use one.
2. On the phone you'll keep in the cab tap **This is the cab phone**; on a spare iPhone tap **This is the sensor phone**. WitMotion sensors just need charging and switching on.
3. Follow the setup steps on the cab phone until you see **READY TO DIG**.
4. To look around first, with one phone: cab phone → Settings → **Practice mode**.

**Recommended sensor**
- **Model:** WitMotion **WT901BLECL** (Bluetooth 5.0). Other models aren't supported yet.
- **How many:** any number from 1 to 4 devices (a device is a WitMotion sensor or the sensor phone; the cab phone isn't counted):
  - **1 on the bucket:** bucket angle; rough air-pressure depth if it's the sensor phone. Move it to the auger drive for auger plumb.
  - **2:** bucket + auger drive (nothing to move), or boom + stick for auger hole depth (about ±2 cm).
  - **3 on boom, stick and bucket:** bucket depth and reach at the teeth, about ±2 cm. The sensor phone is best on the boom. Move the bucket device to the auger drive for hole depth with plumb.
  - **4:** add one on the auger drive: nothing to move, and you can curl freely while drilling.
- **Charging:** USB-C. A charge lasts about a working day, so charge them overnight.
- **Weather:** the sensors aren't waterproof. Fit each WitMotion sensor in a sealed box or case; a sensor phone goes in a case with the bottom edge open. Hold each one firmly so it can't shift.
- **Where to fit the bucket device:** under or on the side of the quick hitch, never on top (it can be pinched at full curl). No hitch? On the back of the bucket, between the ears. Hold it in a case on a rubber-coated pot magnet (at least 25 kg pull), with a backup tie through a hole or tab, so a knock leaves it dangling instead of falling. Mark its outline (or use a small plate it clicks onto) so it goes back at the same angle; if it doesn't, redo **Show the app how the bucket moves**. Curl the bucket fully in and out once and check nothing touches it. Avoid Velcro: heat and dirt make it creep. A sensor phone's case must leave the bottom edge open (that's where it senses air pressure).
- **Where to buy:** WitMotion's official site, [wit-motion.com](https://www.wit-motion.com), or any electronics retailer that sells genuine WitMotion parts.

**Common questions**
- **The phones won't connect.** Turn Bluetooth on for both phones, keep DIG open on the sensor phone, and keep the phones within a few metres for the first link.
- **When do I run setup again?** Settings has three buttons at the top: **Start of day**, **Changed bucket** (after swapping buckets or attachments) and **Full setup** (after moving a phone or sensor).
- **Depth wanders on a windy day.** Phone-only depth uses the barometer and is rough. With the add-on sensors, depth comes from the arm's geometry and isn't affected by wind.
- **Which iPhones work?** iPhone 6s or newer, on iOS 15 or later.

**Please note:** DIG is a guidance aid, not a survey instrument or a safety device. Always check grades with a tape, string line or laser, and locate underground services before you dig (Before You Dig Australia, or 811 in the US).

## Privacy Policy

*Last updated 22 October 2026*

DIG does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- Sensor readings, location and recordings stay on your own devices.
- Location is used only for the compass heading and map on the cab screen, and is never sent anywhere.
- Bluetooth is used only to connect your own phones and sensors.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first. Questions: access.crunchy4y@icloud.com.

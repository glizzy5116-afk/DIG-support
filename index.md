# DIG: Mini Excavator Level
DIG turns iPhones into a simple guidance aid for mini excavators. A spare iPhone becomes the **sensor phone** on the machine, and your everyday iPhone is the **cab phone**. The cab screen shows bucket angle, machine level, auger plumb, trench and slope guides, and a map.

**For accurate depth and reach**, fit 3 or 4 devices on the arm: boom, stick and bucket (plus one on the auger drive for auger work). Each one can be a WitMotion WT901BLECL Bluetooth sensor (bought separately) or your spare iPhone as the sensor phone. Depth comes from the arm's geometry, so it's unaffected by wind.

**Getting started**
1. Install DIG on both iPhones.
2. On the spare phone tap **This is the sensor phone**; on the phone you'll keep in the cab tap **This is the cab phone**.
3. Follow the setup steps on the cab phone until you see **READY TO DIG**.
4. To look around first, with one phone: cab phone → Settings → **Practice mode**.

**Recommended sensor**
- **Model:** WitMotion **WT901BLECL** (Bluetooth 5.0). Other models aren't supported yet.
- **How many:** 3 devices for bucket work (boom, stick, bucket), 4 for auger work (add one on the auger drive; the bucket's device moves to the cradle). Your spare iPhone can be one of them, so most people buy **2 sensors**, or **3** for the auger.
- **Charging:** USB-C. A charge lasts about a working day, so charge them overnight.
- **Weather:** the sensors aren't waterproof. Fit each one in a sealed box or case, held firmly so it can't shift.
- **Where to buy:** WitMotion's official site, [wit-motion.com](https://www.wit-motion.com), or any electronics retailer that sells genuine WitMotion parts.

**Common questions**
- **The phones won't connect.** Turn Bluetooth on for both phones, keep DIG open on the sensor phone, and keep the phones within a few metres for the first link.
- **When do I run setup again?** Settings has three buttons at the top: **Start of day**, **Changed bucket** (after swapping buckets or attachments) and **Full setup** (after moving a phone or sensor).
- **Depth wanders on a windy day.** Phone-only depth uses the barometer and is rough. With the add-on sensors, depth comes from the arm's geometry and isn't affected by wind.
- **Sensors stopped sending.** Charge them overnight; they last about a working day.
- **Which iPhones work?** iPhone 6s or newer, on iOS 15 or later.

**Please note:** DIG is a guidance aid, not a survey instrument or a safety device. Always check grades with a tape, string line or laser, and locate underground services before you dig (Before You Dig Australia, or 811 in the US).

## Privacy Policy

*Last updated 22 October 2026*

DIG does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- Sensor readings, location and recordings stay on your own devices.
- Location is used only for the compass heading and map on the cab screen, and is never sent anywhere.
- Bluetooth is used only to connect your own phones and sensors.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
